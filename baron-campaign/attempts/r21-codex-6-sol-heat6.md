# Baron campaign r21 — cured-era strategy secure, public reel ceiling

The pre-cure r1 strategy, rather than its dead tick stream, should transfer: three T3 sluices fund the turret line, three late decoys buy the burn window, the center decoy alone is repaired, and Blast/rig cycling supplies the missing boss damage.

The public POST refused the compact JSON as `reel_too_large`: the semantic tape was 463,569 bytes against the 64 KiB tape ceiling. Public admission remains blocked by a different capability boundary—the winning standing-order cadence cannot yet be represented under the current reel ceiling without changing its outcome.
